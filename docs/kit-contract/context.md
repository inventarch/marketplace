# Kit contract expectation context

Standing: proposed public expectations, not an installed contract, executed qualification or release permission. These contextual requirements and every case in expectations.json form one review unit. Grammar case numbers and variant IDs are stable cross-reference labels; the complete given/request/expected text is carried here, without a private grammar dependency.

Example-kit labels describe hypothetical fixtures, not observations of a real inventory, release incident or organization. Separate scenarios exercise declared priorities, dependency ordering and required first-group source-package lowering; sharing an example label creates no cross-scenario entity binding. grammarCase and variantId are optional inspection metadata: the required case identity, requirement association and given/request/expected text preserve the obligation. A future manifest must not omit cases lacking optional metadata or rename retained programme variants to make them look uniform.

## Scope and owners

The four contracts describe candidate conformance, qualification inputs, a separate authorized programme audit and checker execution. The proposed content owner is ia-authoring-record and the proposed executing owner is ia-authoring-exec. They use existing public contract/check/case/operation/spec/task vocabulary; this baseline adds no native word or system. Exact native representation and qualified invocation remain open.

ia-authoring-catalogue owns canonical CAT-02/03 release immutability and relevant publication history, CAT-04 executable placement and transition, and CAT-06 authorized publisher-context private-identity screening. The common checker must import exact public definitions by identity, version and digest, without rewriting catalogue policy. Those exact exports and interfaces remain unresolved prerequisites. General public candidate callers need no private identity list or migration inventory. The all-payload executable ownership obligation is broader than the declared-asset/handler subset of CAT-04.

Public and private publication consumers retain their packers, credentials and publication authority and must call the same pinned assessment and final-verification semantics. Programme register instances and accountable participant bindings stay in the authorized workspace; the public work-system owns the work vocabulary. A workspace composes and stores records and grants no authority.

The seven concerns are closure, coverage, selection, ownership, evidence, disclosure and migration; multiple concerns can apply together. Assessment activities are inspect, derive, evaluate, compare and present. Progress (unassessed/evaluating/assessed) belongs to an attempt at an instant; disposition (satisfied/violated/unresolved) belongs to one obligation at identified inputs. Evidence matching is a relation to the assessed tuple, not an intrinsic label on a log. Unassessed means completed assessment standing has not been established. Evaluating means outcomes are still being collected. Assessed means every selected obligation has a disposition, which may include unresolved results. Only all required obligations being satisfied establishes conformance. Historical observations are retained. Visibility, source-transfer eligibility and hosted-invocation eligibility remain independent; no kit name or commercial classification creates an exemption.

## Applicability tiers

Every governance record states its applicability tier and the caller values admitting it. Tier 1 applies to any KB write (the Synthesis move); tier 2 adds the kind of record being written (an artifact-set, kind, category or an exact word where required and supported); tier 3 adds the current activity (phase, primitive, shape or applicable predicate). These are applicability descriptions, not new native vocabulary. A record with no activation is universal and must give an independently reviewed justification and domain-wide delivery evidence. A caller must propagate the coordinates its profile needs. Missing caller data, conservative retention or a claimed but unqualified word distinction cannot prove selective-delivery conformance. Qualify general rules, record-specific rules and activity-specific rules separately, preserving applicable public tier-one expectations. These descriptions do not assert that today's caller or runtime carries every required distinction.

## Derivation and completion

The pinned contract defines a finite versioned set of requirement definitions. The trusted consumer operation fixes the assessment context: candidate release or separate programme audit. Candidate release requires relevant release continuity but never a global private inventory. A programme result cannot satisfy candidate release conformance, and candidate input cannot downgrade the selected context. Unknown context is unresolved. First reconcile every candidate-exposed capability and request entry point, including executable entry bindings, with qualified profile domains. Profiles do not define their own completeness: an uncovered supported request, missing exposure declaration or disagreement between declarations and bindings is unresolved and prevents positive conformance. Intentionally unsupported requests need explicit boundary refusal behavior and matching evidence. Applicability predicates then derive all obligations over the exact closure and reconciled profiles. Private extensions may add obligations but cannot weaken the common baseline.

Each selected obligation retains requirement identity, subject, relevant input identity, expected behavior and result. Missing, unsupported or interrupted evaluation is unresolved; demonstrated failure is violated. Only a completed set of satisfied required obligations yields a conforming assessment. Assessed is not synonymous with conforming.

Results bind candidate, contract and checker and identify evaluator selections and relevant execution inputs. Candidate identity includes the complete frozen materialized payload; the shared final verification binds actual archive and sidecar digests after verifying every byte and declared packaging transformation. Initial release use requires a directly authenticated same-job invocation; edited or portable self-asserted result files do not qualify. A changed semantic input invalidates dependent evidence and aggregate standing. Reuse needs demonstrated dependency equivalence, not a matching label. Assessment success is necessary evidence, not publication authority: the pack/release boundary must compare actual released inputs with assessed inputs and apply its independently owned authorization/availability requirements.

## Falsifiable selection contract

Each evaluation profile (assessment data, not a native agent-profile or routing-profile) declares supported requests, required context, independently reviewed policy-derived oracle and its author/reviewer/content-digest/approval provenance, partition boundaries and environment. Public tier-one expectations come from the pinned contract and cannot be narrowed by candidate data. Self-derived or unreviewed expectations refuse qualification. The surface-to-profile reconciliation must cover the whole candidate's declared and executable request surface before those partitions can establish completion. Qualify inclusion, exclusion, collisions, unknown context and equivalent entry points. Token reduction and selection somewhere are inadequate.

Selectors conservatively retaining a rule on missing coordinates do not establish selective exclusion. Conditioned clauses require matching values and use unique maximum specificity; ties are ambiguous. Test these separately. If populated supported coordinates and resolved subjects still cannot distinguish work requiring different governance, leave the profile unresolved and propose the alternative to IA owners through its public language owner. Never silently approximate policy or treat selection as execution/access permission.

## Layers and runtime changes

First derivation orders declared basis, required assessment, observed assessment and reported standing. Runtime events may create a new candidate or pin selection, append observations or start a new assessment; old observations remain historical. The layer order is not a runtime prohibition on reassessment.

Presentation only reads. A badge, report, catalogue or pointer cannot satisfy an obligation, publish a release, authorize source transfer or permit invocation. Commands from those surfaces pass through the separate authority boundary.

## Proposed execution contract

All common candidate requirements and explicit variants apply. Programme mode is separate and cannot produce a release-conformance receipt. The cases here add runtime profile, bootstrap, provenance and consumer obligations; they do not claim coverage of unrelated programme or private-public dependency cases. Every evaluator remains unavailable.

The exact trusted host/runner version and policy digest select the applicable execution profile. Under the proposed transition requirement only eligible publisher/digest-recorded executing-system assets run through that qualified runner; the qualified binding profile uses actual admitted operation/catalogue joins and invocation. Installed library availability alone establishes neither invocation nor handler retirement. All exposed runner-file and binding surfaces are reconciled. No synthetic published record or relaxed production trust bootstraps the first checker; development test-harness evidence is labelled as such and actual supported bootstrap/consumer qualification is still required by checker qualification.

The shared checker exposes proposed assessment and final-artifact/result-verification operations owned here. Both consumers use these exact semantics. First-version release use accepts direct authenticated invocation results within the same trusted job, not arbitrary portable JSON receipts; cross-job replay for release acceptance is unavailable. Historical evidence can still be retained and a qualified attempt resumed with exact inputs, followed by a fresh same-job final verification.

The qualification inputs below govern canonical inputs, oracle/verifier independence, inert fixtures and materialized payload verification. COMP-COVERAGE counts declared cases, not actual behavior.

## Migration programme assessment shape

This public contract defines an optional audit interface; unrelated public candidate callers do not supply a private global inventory. The authorized programme owner and mandated participant maintain register instances and completion decisions using public work-system vocabulary. Their workspace composes and stores these records; private issue, actor and mandate bindings stay in private instances. ia-authoring-record owns only this public format and assessment semantics; ia-authoring-exec may implement the same pinned generic reconciliation checks in a separately selected programme mode. A release caller cannot choose programme mode to bypass release requirements.

All programme inputs remain in authorized custody. Results explicitly identify programme scope and are never accepted as candidate-conformance receipts. Missing inventory is unresolved, not an empty-inventory success. Implementation and native evidence remain unavailable until the checker and audit interface are qualified. The actual migration programme remains separately tracked by its authorized owner.

## Qualification inputs and trust boundaries

These are proposed normative semantics for the contracts in this design. No operation, result format, signing facility, runtime binding or evaluator is claimed implemented. The input contract in expectations.json carries predicate/provenance behavior; primary REQ-KAC-08 carries lowering equivalence; the other contracts carry payload, invocation, custody and consumer cases. Every concrete variant in expectations.json must survive lowering as its stable case identity and explicit expectations; the next implementation builds the variant manifest from the pinned contract, never from the candidate's list of passing tests.

## Candidate and final artifacts
The candidate is a frozen materialized payload manifest: every release record, asset, runtime module, generated file, executable entry, dependency closure and sidecar has a normalized path or resource identity, length, content digest and data/executable classification. Hosted proprietary recipe payloads remain in publisher custody; their assessment does not distribute them. Exact contract imports, declarative extensions, fixture/oracle definitions, selected runner/profile/trust policy and relevant release-history snapshot are additional identified assessment inputs.

The single checker performs pre-pack assessment against frozen inputs before packaging. Packaging may construct a local unpublished archive; this is not a release or final conformance result. The same pinned checker's final-artifact verification operation reads the actual resulting archive and sidecar bytes, verifies the entire materialized payload plus declared packaging transformations/metadata, and binds archive and artifact-set digests to the result. Unknown generated bytes, extra runtime files or changed sidecars refuse. A source-tree hash alone is never evidence for packed output. First-version transformations must be explicit and verified; no blanket deterministic-pack assumption is accepted. Mutation after sealing requires a new assessment. Both pipeline consumers call this same operation rather than invent independent comparison logic.

## Result authenticity and trust
The trusted release job derives checker/contract/module identities from its exact configured pins and observed invocation, and derives artifact digests from bytes. Candidate data and receipt fields cannot assert trust. Initial release consumers require a direct authenticated checker invocation and final verification within the same trusted job. A copied, relabelled or edited JSON result is not accepted. Cross-job portable receipt acceptance is unavailable until a separately qualified provenance mechanism exists; merely matching fields cannot enable it. Retained historical observations and qualified same-input continuation do not bypass fresh final verification. Publication still checks current independent authority.

Runner profile and trust policy come from the selected host's qualified configuration. The candidate cannot downgrade them. Under the proposed transition requirement the relevant publisher allowlist, recorded digest and executing-system asset rules apply. The cutover record identifies the canonical CAT-04 bindExecution release event for supported execution consumers; it records that event rather than inventing a per-host exemption. After that event the operation/catalogue binding profile applies and handlers.json is refused. A host lacking a qualified applicable path must refuse or remain unresolved, not retain transition indefinitely. CLI/library version alone is not a cutover record. Runner entries in handlers.json, onwrite.json, checks.json and native bindings all belong to surface reconciliation; only handlers.json is subject to the proposed explicit retirement requirement described in this paragraph. The exact canonical CAT-04 export and cutover evidence must still be qualified; this prose is not an accepted policy import. Unsupported runner/profile combinations are unresolved.

## Oracle independence
Selection expectations derive from approved requirement intent, never from the activation or observed output under test. Public tier-one any-KB-write expectations are immutable data in the pinned common contract; candidate/private data cannot narrow them. Each oracle identifies its policy sources, content digest, author, independent reviewer, reviewed revision and approval evidence. The reviewer/verifier must be a distinct authoring execution context that did not create the candidate activation/checker change; relabelling the same run is not independence. A shared accountable human does not make distinct agent review runs identical, but different role names alone do not prove separation. The trusted job authenticates an external repository/workflow approval record supplied by its already authorized service, bound to reviewer identity, execution context, policy basis and approved content digest. The candidate cannot provide that trust anchor. Missing or mismatched provenance refuses.

A person or agent may propose expectations before implementation; an independent reviewer must check them against the governing intent, including activity partitions the candidate omitted. An unreviewed self-derived oracle, absent review identity, contradictory public expectation or mismatched reviewed digest prevents conformance. Actual selectors are compared to this independently reviewed oracle; declaring it independent is insufficient.

## Fixtures and bootstrap
Fixture candidates are inert encoded descriptors with an explicit data content type and digest, not auto-loadable executable files. Malformed/unowned executable examples are materialized only in a controlled scratch test environment and are never auto-executed by data loading. The executable fixture harness is owned by ia-authoring-exec; a tests/ directory never exempts executable bytes from placement policy. Real private recipes or identities are not public fixtures.

Expected outcome changes require a reviewed contract-version change. The checker must retain and pass the pinned prior fixture baseline for unchanged supported contract semantics; removals or changed expectations need an explicit reviewed versioned delta, not silent rebaselining. Evidence binds fixture baseline, oracle approvals, checker revision, actual outcomes and independent verifier identity/run. The checker steward cannot approve its own bootstrap by changing expectations beside code.

An unpublished candidate can be tested by an explicitly authorized development harness without pretending to be a published trusted kit. Those observations are labelled development evidence; they do not satisfy production-runner publisher/recorded-digest eligibility or grant adoption. No synthetic published release row, trust allowlist relaxation or implicit first-release exemption is permitted. The admissible first-release class is restricted to the canonical ia-authoring release qualifying its own embedded checker version. Its already authorized maintainer build job independently verifies source/publication identity, exact module inclusion and digest, then invokes the reviewed unpublished checker through its qualified operation runner. This self-bootstrap receipt cannot qualify example-kit or any other kit. Ordinary-kit release consumers must verify the exact checker module pin against an accepted published canonical ia-authoring release and required contract policy; local forks or reviewed-but-unadopted builds refuse even in trusted jobs. This route uses repository/workflow authorization to run build inputs; it is not installed-plugin transition invocation and does not relax that plugin publisher/digest policy. Candidate or fork-job assertions cannot select trust. The implementation must qualify this route and consumer refusal behavior before checker qualification completes. Missing bootstrap support remains an implementation limitation, not a reason to report green.

## Extensions and offline custody
Initial private extensions are versioned declarative data interpreted by the same public checker, not callbacks. The public content owner defines a finite reviewed predicate language; the executing owner implements it. Required demonstrated operations are field equality, identifier pattern, membership, cardinality, required/forbidden record or asset presence and reference/composition constraints. Each needs explicit missing-value, malformed-input and boundary tests before use; unsupported semantics or versions remain unresolved. In a hypothetical extension scenario, a kit owner supplies private rule data locally; any legacy seat wording must be reconciled against the exact public catalogue CAT-07 definition before encoding. No private script is substituted when a predicate is unavailable.

The caller supplies a complete authorized local closure. Validation may not silently query a registry, send telemetry or disclose private coordinates to repair missing input. Missing local private data is unresolved. Any explicitly permitted public retrieval is a separate host-authorized step before freezing inputs, with no private query material. Reports have explicit audience and field disclosure policy; private-identity publication screening and global migration inventory remain separate scoped obligations.

## Release sequencing
The proposed release requirement is that every new release conform. Until the common contract/checker and its applicable invocation path are qualified, affected new releases—including any hypothetical urgent or priority re-cut—cannot truthfully pass that requirement. Existing immutable releases remain available. No before-rollout, urgency or bootstrap exemption is implied. The implementation must make preflight and final verification fail closed in both release consumers before accepting new cuts; an exception would require an explicit owner ruling, which this design does not request or assume.

## Current checker eligibility
ia-authoring-record maintains the public gate-eligibility policy with required contract/checker minimums, explicitly withdrawn-as-gate digests, monotonically identified policy revisions and an explicit validity/freshness condition. The trusted publication consumer authenticates the policy through its established publisher/workflow trust anchor, checks its validity against trusted host time/known revision, and binds the snapshot to the acceptance attempt. The candidate cannot select or sign its own policy. Offline checking may use a valid authenticated prefetched public snapshot; missing or expired freshness evidence fails closed rather than querying with private data. No arbitrary validity period is invented here; its concrete format and enforcement require qualification. Historical releases remain immutable and served even when no longer eligible to gate new releases.

Current eligibility comes from a separately published monotonically revisioned public channel owned by ia-authoring-record, independently selected by the trusted consumer, never the pinned checker release. A release may carry the policy schema or historical provenance only. Every acceptance requires either a snapshot with a finite enforced expiry or a fresh per-acceptance authority check; until-superseded and unbounded validity are forbidden. The concrete transport, finite period, trust anchor and enforcement remain checker qualification work. Offline public prefetch must satisfy the same finite expiry and rollback checks.

Any policy revision that lowers a floor, removes a withdrawal or otherwise widens checker eligibility requires an authenticated independent approval record with stated reason, exact previous/new policy digests and supporting requalification evidence under REQ-KACI-02. Publisher authentication alone is insufficient. Tightening-only revisions may use the established single authority; neither path grants an urgency waiver or changes contract requirements.

The public baseline is an explicitly reviewed public projection, never a verbatim publication of this private design worktree. Public contract text, cases, provenance links and qualification identities use generic or synthetic labels and public identifiers only. Private programme bindings, issue references, accountable identities and inventory stay in private instances. The authorized publisher performs existing CAT-06 private-identity screening before baseline publication; its sensitive identity list remains local, not a public checker dependency. Independent review verifies that replacing private labels preserves all four contract identities and every requirement, case/context and expected behavior; redaction cannot drop or weaken a case. Missing safe publication evidence refuses the public baseline.

Every withdrawal records its reason and exact affected digests. For a behavioral defect, the reviewed versioned fixture delta must include a reproducing case, and removal requires independently verified passing evidence for that case as well as the retained baseline. Rerunning the pre-defect baseline alone cannot remove a withdrawal. Non-behavioral withdrawals require reason-specific resolution evidence; an unresolved reason or absent evidence cannot be waived by a policy approval.

Native drafts for public adoption are lowered from the exact reviewed public projection, never directly from private design cards. The authorized publisher applies CAT-06 screening to both that baseline and the resulting lowered records/final public artifacts; passing the earlier projection screen does not exempt generated output.
